Official EthioCosmos profile

EthioCosmos Learning Community

EthioCosmos is a student-led astronomy and space-science platform created and founded by Henok Girma, a student at Dodola Ifa Boru Special Boarding Secondary School in Ethiopia.

EthioCosmos was mainly founded to make astronomy and space science more accessible to Ethiopian learners, especially by providing educational content in native Ethiopian languages. It currently supports Amharic, while work is underway to expand the platform to Tigrigna, Afaan Oromo, Somali, and other Ethiopian languages.

The website helps learners explore topics such as planets, stars, galaxies, black holes, nebulae, astrobiology, space missions, extraterrestrial life, and future space exploration. It also highlights astronomy and space science from an Ethiopian perspective.

EthioCosmos also has a mobile app version, designed to provide a more convenient and interactive learning experience. The app includes astronomy lessons, quizzes, space news, Shorts, Live content, a 3D Solar System, featured space topics, and an AI Assistant.

EthioCosmos is more than a website—it is a student-led learning community aiming to bring the universe closer to Ethiopian learners through accessible, engaging, and multilingual science education.
