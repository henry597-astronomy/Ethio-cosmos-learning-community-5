# EthioCosmos Project Knowledge Base

This document summarizes the core vision, mission, and technical requirements of the EthioCosmos project as defined in the official proposal (June 2026).

## 1. Project Overview
*   **Project Name:** Ethio Cosmos Learning Community
*   **Target Audience:** Ethiopian teenagers and young adults (ages 13–20).
*   **Core Goal:** Bridge the gap in astronomy and space science education in Ethiopia through an interactive, community-driven digital platform.
*   **Institution:** Dodola Ifa Boru Special Boarding Secondary School.

## 2. Mission & Vision
*   **Mission:** Providing a comprehensive, multilingual (English, Amharic, Afan Oromo), and highly interactive learning platform focused on astronomy.
*   **Vision:** Empowering every Ethiopian teenager to explore the cosmos and drive innovation within their communities.

## 3. Core Features & Functionality
*   **Structured Curriculum:** Organized astronomy lessons with downloadable study materials.
*   **Progress Tracking:** Interactive tests and academic growth visualization.
*   **Smart AI Assistant:** On-demand tutoring powered by Groq AI.
*   **Social Learning Feed:** A TikTok-style feed for short-form educational videos.
*   **Live Collaboration:** High-fidelity community chats and "Live Host" virtual meeting sessions (powered by LiveKit).

## 4. Technical Stack
*   **Frontend:** React (Next.js/Vite) + TypeScript.
*   **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, RLS).
*   **Hosting:** Vercel.
*   **Real-time:** LiveKit (Streaming/Meetings).
*   **AI:** Groq (Whisper for voice, Llama/Mixtral for chat).
*   **Mobile:** Capacitor (Android wrapper with PWA/Offline capabilities).

## 5. Future Roadmap
1.  **Multilingual Support:** Full integration of Amharic and Afan Oromo.
2.  **Educational Scope Expansion:** Moving beyond astronomy to general science disciplines.
3.  **Android Optimization:** Enhancing offline access and native performance for regional connectivity challenges.
